This edition's theme

Metamorphosis

Every idea worth sharing starts somewhere uncertain. It could be a question, a doubt, a habit we want to change, or simply the feeling that things could be different. Metamorphosis is about the space between who we were and who we are becoming. It is that moment when an old way of thinking no longer feels right, but the next one has not quite taken shape yet.

We chose this theme because all of us are changing in some way. Every speaker on our stage has a story of something they questioned, challenged, or chose to see differently. The same is true for everyone sitting in the audience. We are all learning, unlearning, growing, and finding our own way forward.

This is not a theme about having everything figured out. It is about the process of getting there, through the questions we ask, the mistakes we make, and the moments that change how we see ourselves and the world around us. Metamorphosis is about becoming, and finding meaning in the process.

The format

What is TEDx?

TED is a nonprofit organisation dedicated to sharing ideas that can spark curiosity, challenge perspectives, and inspire meaningful conversations. TED talks are known for taking powerful ideas and making them accessible through concise, compelling storytelling.

TEDx brings this spirit to local communities. These events are independently organised under a licence from TED, following its standards while allowing each community to shape an event around its own people, ideas, and perspectives.

The “x” stands for independently organised TED event. TED provides the framework; the community provides the voice. That means a TEDx stage can give a platform to ideas that begin anywhere — including within our own schools and communities.

Our event

About TEDxWittyIntlSchoolYouth

This is the first edition of TEDxWittyIntlSchoolYouth, a youth-led TEDx event created to bring student voices, fresh ideas, and honest conversations onto one stage. Organised by students, for students, the event is built around a simple belief: a good idea does not need a certain age, title, or position to deserve to be heard.

As our first edition, this is the beginning of something we hope to see grow over the years. Our aim is to create a space where young people can speak openly about the ideas and experiences that matter to them, while giving others the chance to listen, question, and see things from a different perspective.
